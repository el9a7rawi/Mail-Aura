Privacy Policy and Disclosure

Mail Aura has a single purpose: verify email addresses and clean email lists. It does not collect data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond that.

Data the extension handles

  • Email addresses. Sent to public verification services only when you request a check. They are not stored permanently. The single exception is a context-menu result, which is held in storage.session until the popup reads it and then removed immediately; results older than one minute are discarded.
  • Page content. Visible email addresses are read only when you press Scan This Page. Browsing history is not tracked.
  • Settings. Your theme, privacy mode, and display options are stored locally through chrome.storage. No account is created.

External services

By default, checks use eight free, keyless public services over HTTPS: Rapid Email Validator, Disify, DeBounce, MailCheck.ai, Disban, EmailProbe, and DNS-over-HTTPS from Google and Cloudflare.

Privacy mode stops all of these instantly; verification becomes local-only. The status line confirms the change when you toggle it.

Fonts are bundled inside the extension (Inter, Cairo and Tajawal); no font request leaves the browser.

Permissions

  • activeTab + scripting. Used only to scan the page you are on when you press Scan.
  • storage. Keeps your settings and the temporary context-menu result.
  • contextMenus. Adds Verify this email address to selections and mailto links.
  • sidePanel. Opens the wider results view for long lists.

Incognito

No results from private browsing are kept in persistent storage. A context-menu result is removed from storage.session as soon as it is read and does not survive a browser restart.

Removing data

Clear results removes every displayed result and the domain-level cache immediately. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored settings.

Compliance: data received is used only for the stated purpose, in line with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
